# MenStyle Market — Product Catalog

## Overview

The MenStyle Market product catalog organizes men's fashion products into five main categories.

## Product Categories

- Streetwear
- Suits
- Watches
- Shoes
- Accessories

## Main Features

- Browse available products.
- Filter products by category.
- View detailed product information.
- Display product prices and descriptions.
